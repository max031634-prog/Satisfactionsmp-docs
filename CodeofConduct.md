Code of Conduct

Server Name: SatisfactionSMP (SFS)
1. Our Commitment

SatisfactionSMP is dedicated to providing a safe, fair, and welcoming environment for all players, regardless of their background, experience level, or playstyle. We value a healthy competitive spirit, mutual respect, and positive community interactions above all else.
2. Core Values

  Fair Play: Play honestly. Cheating, exploiting bugs, or using unauthorized modifications ruins the experience for everyone and will not be tolerated.

  Respect and Inclusivity: Treat all community members with respect. Discrimination, harassment, and targeted toxicity have no place on our server or our Discord.

  Constructive Communication: Keep chat clean and helpful. Disagreements happen, but they should be handled maturely and without personal attacks or abuse.

  Community Collaboration: We grow together. Report bugs, share feedback constructively, and help newer players find their footing.

3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by SatisfactionSMP, including:

  The official Minecraft server.

  The official Discord server and voice channels.

  Associated websites, forms, and social media platforms operated by our staff.

4. Enforcement and Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be reviewed by the staff team and may result in:

  A formal warning.

  Temporary mutes or suspensions from chat or the server.

  Permanent bans from SatisfactionSMP and all associated platforms in severe or repeat cases.

5. Reporting Issues

If you witness someone breaking this Code of Conduct, experiencing harassment, or exploiting the server, please report it immediately to our staff team via our official Discord support channels. Help us keep SatisfactionSMP a great place to play!
