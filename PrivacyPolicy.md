Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Server Name: SatisfactionSMP (SFS)
1. Overview

At SatisfactionSMP, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data that is collected while you play on our Minecraft server, visit our website, or interact with our Discord community. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.
2. Information We Collect

When you interact with SatisfactionSMP, we may automatically or manually collect certain information:

  Minecraft Account Data: Your Minecraft username, unique player UUID, and skin data required for authentication and gameplay. (Please note that Minecraft usernames and UUIDs are public information visible to others on the server and via public Minecraft tools).

   Server Logs: In-game actions, chat logs, IP addresses, and connection timestamps necessary for server security, moderation, and anti-cheat enforcement.

   Discord Data: Your Discord username, user ID, and messages sent within our community server if you choose to join and participate.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

   To operate, maintain, and secure the SatisfactionSMP server.

   To enforce our server rules, investigate rule breaks, and handle staff reports or support tickets.

   To prevent cheating, exploitation, ban evasion, and malicious attacks against our infrastructure.

   To improve server performance and community features.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

    No Selling of Data: We will never sell, trade, or rent your personal data or Minecraft information to third parties.

    Service Providers: Data may be processed by essential hosting providers and security tools required to keep the server online (such as our game server host and database management systems).

    Legal Obligations: We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety, rights, and property of SatisfactionSMP, our staff, and our community members.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical precautions to secure our server files, databases, and logs against unauthorized access. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within our documentation or announced on our Discord server. Your continued use of our services after updates constitutes your agreement to the revised policy.
7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please reach out to our administration team via our official Discord server.
